# Product Templates System - Business PRD

## Executive Summary

Product Templates is a centralized insurance product management system that enables insurance professionals to create, organize, and deploy standardized product offerings across their entire business ecosystem. The system serves as the single source of truth for all insurance products, allowing teams to maintain consistency while providing the flexibility to customize products for specific customer, partner, and opportunity relationships.

## 1. Business Problem & Opportunity

### 1.1 Current Challenges
- **Inconsistent Product Information**: Different teams maintain separate product lists leading to data discrepancies
- **Time-Intensive Setup**: Creating product assignments for each customer/partner relationship is manual and repetitive
- **Limited Visibility**: Difficulty tracking which products are assigned to which entities across the organization
- **Scaling Issues**: As product portfolio grows, managing individual product relationships becomes unmanageable

### 1.2 Market Opportunity
- **Efficiency Gains**: Reduce product setup time by 75% through standardized templates
- **Data Quality**: Ensure consistent product information across all business relationships
- **Scalability**: Support unlimited product growth without proportional administrative overhead
- **Revenue Impact**: Faster product deployment enables quicker time-to-market for new offerings

## 3. Target Users & Use Cases

### 3.1 Primary Users

#### Insurance Product Managers
**Role**: Maintain the master product catalog and ensure data accuracy  
**Primary Needs**: 
- Create comprehensive product templates with all necessary attributes
- Organize products in logical hierarchies for easy discovery
- Update product information that automatically propagates to all relationships

#### Account Managers
**Role**: Assign products to customers and manage ongoing relationships  
**Primary Needs**:
- Quickly find and assign appropriate products to customers
- Track which products each customer has
- Customize product attributes for specific customer needs

#### Partnership Managers
**Role**: Manage product offerings available through broker and partner channels  
**Primary Needs**:
- Configure product availability for different partner types
- Track partner product preferences and performance
- Maintain consistent product information across partner network

### 3.2 Core Business Scenarios

#### Scenario 1: New Product Launch
**Situation**: Insurance company launches new health insurance product  
**Current Process**: Each team manually creates product entries, leading to inconsistencies  
**With Product Templates**: Product Manager creates single template, all teams access standardized information

#### Scenario 2: Customer Product Assignment
**Situation**: Account Manager needs to assign multiple products to new enterprise customer  
**Current Process**: Manual entry of each product with risk of errors and omissions  
**With Product Templates**: Quick selection from organized template library with automatic data population

#### Scenario 3: Partner Product Catalog
**Situation**: Broker partner requests updated product catalog for their sales team  
**Current Process**: Manual compilation of product information from various sources  
**With Product Templates**: Automated generation of current product catalog with real-time information

## 7. Implementation Approach

### 7.1 Rollout Strategy

#### Phase 1: Foundation (Month 1-2)
- Deploy core product template functionality
- Migrate existing product data to new system
- Train product management team on template creation
- Establish initial product hierarchy and organization

#### Phase 2: Team Expansion (Month 3-4)
- Roll out to account management teams
- Implement product assignment workflows
- Deploy relationship tracking capabilities
- Conduct comprehensive user training sessions

#### Phase 3: Full Deployment (Month 5-6)
- Extend access to all relevant teams
- Activate advanced reporting and analytics
- Implement integration with existing business systems
- Launch ongoing support and maintenance procedures

### 7.2 Change Management

#### User Training
- **Role-Specific Training**: Customized training for different user types
- **Hands-On Workshops**: Practical sessions with real business scenarios
- **Documentation**: Comprehensive user guides and quick reference materials
- **Ongoing Support**: Dedicated help resources and regular training updates

#### Communication Plan
- **Executive Briefings**: Regular updates to leadership on adoption and benefits
- **Team Communications**: Weekly updates during rollout with tips and success stories
- **Success Sharing**: Highlight teams and individuals achieving efficiency gains
- **Feedback Channels**: Multiple ways for users to provide input and suggestions

### 7.3 Risk Mitigation

#### Data Migration
- **Comprehensive Testing**: Extensive validation of data transfer accuracy
- **Backup Procedures**: Complete backup of existing data before migration
- **Parallel Operation**: Run old and new systems simultaneously during transition
- **Rollback Plan**: Ability to revert to previous system if critical issues arise

#### User Adoption
- **Champion Program**: Identify and train power users in each team
- **Incentive Structure**: Recognize teams that successfully adopt the new system
- **Gradual Transition**: Phased approach allowing teams to adapt progressively
- **Continuous Improvement**: Regular system updates based on user feedback
