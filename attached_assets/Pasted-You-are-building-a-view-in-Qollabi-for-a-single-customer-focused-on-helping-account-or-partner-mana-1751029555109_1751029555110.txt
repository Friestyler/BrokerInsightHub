You are building a view in Qollabi for a single customer, focused on helping account or partner managers identify upsell and cross-sell opportunities within that customer’s insurance portfolio.

⸻

🎯 Objective

Design an interface that clearly shows:
	1.	The insurance product categories that apply to this customer
	2.	The products the customer already has in each category
	3.	The contract status and expiration date for each product
	4.	Contracts expiring soon (e.g. within the next 90 days)
	5.	Cross-sell opportunities within the same category (other products the customer doesn’t yet have — but do not label this as “white space” yet)
	6.	Suggested actions the user can take:
	•	Renew an expiring contract
	•	Propose an additional product
	•	Offer a bundle

This is not a general analytics dashboard. It is a focused, actionable view of one customer’s insurance portfolio.

⸻

🧩 Input Data

You’ll be working with this structure:
	•	Customer (Context: this entire view is about one customer)
	•	Categories (e.g. Health, Legal, Disability)
	•	Products (e.g. “Legal Basic”, “Income Protection Plus”)
	•	Belongs to a Category
	•	Can be Active, Expiring, Renewed, Cancelled
	•	Has a Contract End Date
	•	May include Tags (e.g. Strategic, High-Margin, Bundleable)
	•	May be linked to a suggested bundle or complementary product

Note: A customer can only have one instance of a product, but multiple products per category are possible.

⸻

🧠 Interface Design Guidance
	•	Use shadcn/ui components only
	•	Design with the minimalist clarity of Google or Apple-style interfaces (clean spacing, subtle hierarchy, sharp typography)
	•	Prioritize readability and scan-ability for a human reviewing a portfolio
	•	Make it easy to spot:
	•	What’s active
	•	What’s expiring
	•	What else could be proposed

⸻

🔧 Layout Suggestions (you’re free to explore)
	•	Table or Card Layout, grouped by category
	•	Each row = one product
	•	Columns = status, expiration date, suggested action
	•	Inline actions: “Renew”, “Propose”, “Bundle”
	•	Visual cues: badges, urgency chips, colored tags for expiring soon
	•	Optional: add filters or dropdowns for category, status, tag
