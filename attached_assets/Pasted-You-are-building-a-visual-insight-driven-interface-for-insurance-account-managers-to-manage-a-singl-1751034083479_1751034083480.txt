You are building a visual, insight-driven interface for insurance account managers to manage a single customer’s insurance portfolio.

This is not a data report. This is a decision cockpit — designed to answer:

⸻

🔍 Core Questions:
	1.	✅ What coverage does the customer already have?
	2.	✅ Which policies require renewal or upgrade?
	3.	✅ What additional products could be added now (cross-sell)?
	4.	✅ Which existing policies could be upgraded (upsell)?
	5.	✅ Where are white space gaps based on the customer’s profile and common product combinations?
	6.	✅ What’s the total premium uplift potential?

⸻

🧩 MANDATORY LAYOUT SECTIONS & VISUALS

⸻

🟦 1. Coverage Overview by Category

📌 Required Visual: Horizontal Bar Chart or Radial Meter Grid

For each product category, show:
	•	% of products currently held
	•	% not held
	•	Total number of products in that category

DO NOT use cards, lists, or tables here.

Use either:
	•	✅ Radial progress charts per category (1 per category, side by side), or
	•	✅ Stacked horizontal bar chart, grouped by category

Color code:
	•	✅ Green = Covered
	•	⚠️ Orange = Partially covered
	•	❌ Gray = Not covered

This section gives a glanceable sense of overall portfolio shape across categories.

⸻

🟥 2. Policies Requiring Attention

📌 Required Visual: Timeline Strip or Vertical Priority Stack

Create a visually ranked stack (or horizontal timeline) of:
	•	Expiring contracts (within 30–90 days)
	•	Policies that can be upgraded
	•	Bundle-eligible products

Each visual block must include:
	•	Product name
	•	Expiry date
	•	Urgency indicator (🔴 19 days left)
	•	Suggested action: Renew, Upgrade, Bundle
	•	Expected premium impact

🛑 Do not use a plain card list. This must be visually structured by priority or urgency.

⸻

🟨 3. Smart Coverage Gaps (Light White Space Logic)

📌 Required Visual: Strategic Recommendation Cards

You must build a “Smart Suggestions” panel with 3–6 recommended product additions, based on:
	•	Customer profile
	•	Bundle eligibility
	•	Peer usage or industry norms
	•	Time events (there is a flood in the customer's region, or it's that time of the year to pay taxes, or something that would resonate like this)

Each card must include:
	•	Missing product name
	•	Business reason: e.g. “Common in similar profiles,” “Part of Financial Protection bundle,” “Regulatory gap”. Explain in 1-2 sentences.
	•	Estimated premium uplift
	•	CTA: Prepare Quote, Add to Proposal

✅ These are profile-based white space opportunities — not a raw product dump.

DO NOT skip this section. This is where the system becomes advisory.

⸻

🟧 4. Uncovered Products Chart

📌 Required Visual: Bar Chart with Filtering

You must build a bar chart that shows, per category:
	•	Total number of uncovered products

Each bar = one category. Bar height = number of products not held in that category.

✅ Required filters above the chart:
	•	Product(s)
	•	Category
	•	Average Price Range (use slider or predefined buckets: €0–€1K, €1K–€5K, €5K+)

Use consistent colors across bars (e.g., all in neutral blue tones).

This is not optional and must be implemented as a visual, not as a table or tag cloud.

⸻

🟩 5. Total Premium Uplift Panel

📌 Required Visual: Large Summary Block with Visual Number Emphasis

Create a fixed or bottom-pinned block showing:
	•	“Estimated premium uplift from all proposed actions: +€[amount]/year”
	•	Subtext: % growth vs current portfolio (if available)

Use visual emphasis:
	•	Bold type
	•	Light animated count-up
	•	Color-coded change arrow (e.g. green ↑)

This gives business justification for every action above.

⸻

🎨 Design & Component Instructions
	•	Use shadcn/ui components only
	•	Style must reflect a Google/Apple design ethos:
	•	Clarity
	•	Strong visual hierarchy
	•	Minimal distraction
	•	Use color only to reinforce urgency, fit, or coverage status
	•	Use icons, badges, progress indicators, not raw text or flat UI blocks

⸻

❌ DO NOT
	•	Use tables, plain lists, or generic cards for coverage or opportunity sections
	•	Make any visual or graph optional
	•	Overload the UI with filters (except for the uncovered product chart)

⸻

✅ Success =

A portfolio manager opens the page and within 15 seconds can:
	•	See where the customer is covered or not
	•	Know which contracts require immediate action
	•	Understand what new or upgraded products to offer — and why
	•	View the premium uplift they could unlock

They should never need to analyze a table to take smart action.
