Customer Portfolio Decision Interface for Insurance Account Managers
(Focus on coverage, renewals, upsell/cross-sell)

⸻

You are designing a visual decision interface for an insurance account manager, focused on a single customer’s insurance portfolio.

This is not a reporting dashboard. It is a portfolio cockpit that helps the user know where to act now, why, and what the impact is — without needing to analyze data manually.

⸻

🔍 Key Business Questions
	1.	What product categories is the customer already covered for?
	2.	Which contracts are expiring soon?
	3.	What additional products or upgrades are most relevant to propose now (cross-sell & upsell)?
	4.	What’s the projected increase in written premium from those actions?

⸻

🧩 Layout Structure (STRICT)

⸻

1. 🟦 Coverage Summary by Category

🛑 Do not use tables or cards here.
✅ You must use non-tabular visualizations to display this.

Use one of the following:
	•	✅ Radial (donut) charts
	•	✅ Horizontal bar meters
	•	✅ Segmented ring visuals

Each visual should show, per category:
	•	% of products currently held
	•	% not held (uncovered)
	•	overlay premium value or potential uplift
	•	Use color coding: green = covered, gray = uncovered, yellow/red = action needed

This gives a glanceable pulse on portfolio strength by category.
📌 If you skip this visual format, the feature fails its goal.

⸻

2. 🟥 Policies Requiring Attention

Show only the most urgent contracts that are:
	•	Expiring within 30–90 days
	•	Up for renewal or upgrade
	•	At risk of churn or downgrade

Use a compact timeline bar or stacked visual cards with urgency indicators.
Each visual card must include:
	•	Product name
	•	Expiration date
	•	Current status
	•	Suggested action: e.g., Add to list of opportunities to share with partner, Add to campaign, ...
	•	Estimated value impact (premium uplift)

Use red/yellow urgency badges (e.g., 🔴 19 days left).

⸻

3. 🟨 Coverage Expansion Paths

Present a prioritized, insight-driven list of next best actions. This must include both:
	•	Cross-sell: new products the customer doesn’t yet have
	•	Upsell: upgrades to existing coverages (e.g., Standard → Premium, add-ons, riders)

You must group or rank them by:
	•	💰 Premium value
	•	⏰ Urgency
	•	✅ Profile fit or eligibility

Each opportunity card must include:
	•	Why it’s relevant now (e.g., “Auto + Home, but no Legal – common coverage trio”)
	•	Expected annual premium uplift
	•	CTA: Prepare Quote, Propose Coverage, Schedule Call

✅ Use insurance-native terms: Add coverage, Upgrade policy, Mitigate exposure — not “marketing” or “campaign” terms.

⸻

4. 🟩 Premium Impact Panel

📌 Required at the bottom or pinned right.

Show a clear summary of:
	•	Total potential premium uplift from all proposed actions (e.g., +€9,300/year)
	•	Visualize this as a meter, badge, or animated number
	•	This helps anchor all actions in business value

⸻

🎨 Design Guidelines
	•	Use shadcn/ui components
	•	Style must be clean and clear, like Apple or Google dashboards
	•	Avoid color overload — use meaningful visual hierarchy (icons, indicators, spacing)
	•	✨ Every visual must be designed to help the user decide faster, not read longer

⸻

❌ DO NOT
	•	Use tables, generic product cards, or passive lists
	•	Show full product catalogs or generic grids
	•	Include aggregated data across customers

⸻

✅ Success =

An insurance account manager opens the view and immediately sees:
	•	Where the customer has strong or weak coverage
	•	Which contracts need urgent attention
	•	Which upgrades or add-ons are worth offering now
	•	What the total business impact will be — without needing to analyze anything
