🎯 Your Goal
Design clean, visual elements that show the customer’s current insurance product portfolio, with immediate clarity on:
1. Which categories the customer is active in
2. Which products they have per category
3. Which contracts are expiring soon (e.g. within 90 days)
4. Which complementary products could be added
5. What the next best commercial action is (renew, new products, launch a campaign, share with a partner)

Interface Rules
• Do NOT use long lists
• Use visualizations to drive insight:
• Radial charts, progress rings, punch cards, bar segments, category grids, or timelines
• Think: Apple Health, Google Finance, Stripe Analytics
• Emphasize:
• Coverage per category (visually show what’s present and how much isn't)
• Contract timeline per product (e.g. expiring soon = red, renewed = green)
• Cross & up-sell potential: show empty or suggestive product spaces subtly (no “white space” label yet)
• Prioritize clarity, hierarchy, elegance
• Add inline action cards or buttons like:
• "View list of opportunities" "Share list of opportunities with partner" "Add to a campaign"

✨ Suggested Components & Ideas (but feel free to innovate)
• Category grid with coverage rings: show what % of that category is covered by the customer
• Horizontal timeline slider: showing contract end dates over the next 12 months
• Bubble chart or chord diagram: to connect owned products with upsell suggestions
• Summary callout blocks:
• “2 contracts expiring in 30 days”
• “Customer has 1 product in Disability – consider x”

🧭 Design Style
• Use shadcn/ui components
• Match the Google or Apple aesthetic: minimal, clean, tactile
• Think visuals for action, not admin report
• Every element should answer a question/ provoke a follow-up

An interface where someone can see a beautiful and visual portfolio snapshot, and say:
👉 “Ah, I see what the cross and upsell opportunities are at this customer”
👉 “They only have one product in Health – I should suggest X,”
👉 “Let’s push that bundle before end of quarter.”
