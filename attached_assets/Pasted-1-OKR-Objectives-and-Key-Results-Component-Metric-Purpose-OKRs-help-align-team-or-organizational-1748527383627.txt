C. Realized

Can be optional or required

The value in the realized field can be updated manually by the users (considering they have permission to do so), by uploading data into Qollabi AI via an Excel or CSV file, or through a data integration.

 

This field is connected to:

Measure unit: The format of the realized field will change depending on the Measure unit selected.

Target: Realized is measured against the target.

Progress: How much, in %, is the realized out of the target.

Traffic light: How is the realized doing against the target in colored statuses.

Trend chart: Indicates every entry of the realized in a trend-line graph.

Responsible: To identify who needs to work on the OKR.

Due date: To identify when the realized value should reach the target.

Milestone: Represents the amount of times the realized value should reach the target.

History: Log every time the realized changes, either manually or via a data integration.
