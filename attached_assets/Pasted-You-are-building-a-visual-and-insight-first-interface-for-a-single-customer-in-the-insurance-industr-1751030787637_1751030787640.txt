You are building a visual and insight-first interface for a single customer in the insurance industry.
This is not a report. It’s a decision cockpit.

Your goal: Help account managers immediately understand:
	•	Which product category offers the most upsell/cross-sell value
	•	Where time should be spent this quarter
	•	What actions to take right now, and why

⸻

🎯 Business Logic

This customer:
	•	Has multiple product categories (e.g., Life, Non-Life, Services)
	•	Can only buy one product per type, but multiple types per category
	•	Has contract statuses (Active, Expiring, Cancelled, Renewed)
	•	May qualify for bundles or upsells

⸻

💥 Interface Must Show

✅ 1. Category-Level Comparison at the Top

Make the system tell them where the gold is.

Design a Category Prioritization Visual (bar chart, bubble chart, quadrant, radar):

For each category:
	•	💰 Total Upsell Potential (e.g. €12K in Life)
	•	⏳ Urgency Level (e.g. 2 contracts expiring soon)
	•	📈 Customer Under-coverage (products missing, visually – no white space logic yet)
	•	🧭 Recommended Focus Score (generated from the above)

Let the user scan and know in 5 seconds: “OK, Legal Services needs my attention this week.”

⸻

✅ 2. Top Actions to Take – No Thinking Required

A “Next Best Actions” panel with 3–5 clear suggestions like:
	•	“Renew Auto Insurance – 19 days left”
	•	“Propose Business Insurance – 78% conversion rate, €4,500 potential”
	•	“Send bundle offer for Travel + Life before June 30”

Add urgency indicators: 🔥 ⚠️ ⏰ 💡

⸻

✅ 3. Strategic Cross-Sell Opportunities

Grouped by impact, not category. Prioritize:
	•	💸 High value
	•	⚠️ Time-sensitive
	•	✅ Easy wins (bundle-matching, high conversion profiles)

Visually highlight reasons to act. Not just “add to campaign” — show why this is worth it now.

⸻

🧩 Design Rules
	•	No tables or passive lists
	•	Use shadcn/ui components, styled like Google/Apple
	•	Prefer:
	•	Bar/radar/punch card for category comparison
	•	Timeline strips for contract urgency
	•	Card stacks for prioritized cross-sell
	•	Callout blocks for insights like “Most untapped value in Services”
	•	Use colors, chips, badges, icons to encode meaning instantly
(e.g. red = urgent, green = stable, blue = high potential)

⸻

❌ Strictly Avoid
	•	Flat lists of products or contracts
	•	Treating all categories equally
	•	Forcing users to manually compare or dig
	•	Showing missing products as white space

⸻

🧠 Ask Yourself as the Agent
	•	“If I had 10 minutes with this account, where would I act first?”
	•	“How can I visually rank where their biggest opportunity lies?”
	•	“What insight would make the manager say: ‘Aha – that’s what I’ll do next.’”

⸻

🎯 Success =

A product manager or partner manager opens this view and knows exactly what to prioritize, why, and how to act — without needing to analyze anything.
