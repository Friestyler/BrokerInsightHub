Customer Portfolio Decision Interface for Insurance Account Managers
(No white space logic yet — focus on coverage, renewals, and next best coverage)

⸻

You are designing a visual decision interface for insurance account managers. The view is for a single customer and should help answer:

🔍 Key Questions:
	1.	What insurance coverage does this customer already have?
	2.	Where are there critical gaps in their protection?
	3.	Which contracts are expiring soon and require action?
	4.	What additional coverages are most relevant to offer now, and why?
	5.	What’s the estimated uplift in written premium from those offers?

⸻

🎯 Business Context
	•	The customer can hold multiple products, grouped into categories (e.g., Life, Non-Life, Services).
	•	Contracts have status: Active, Expiring, Cancelled, Renewed.
	•	Products may belong to common bundles (e.g., Life + Pension).
	•	Some gaps may indicate risk exposure or regulatory obligations.

It’s a portfolio management cockpit for a account managers.

⸻

🧩 Layout Structure & Component Guidelines

1. 🟦 Coverage Summary by Category

Use a clean visual overview to compare product coverage per category. For each category, show:
	•	✅ Number of active products
	•	⚪️ Number of available products not yet held (but don’t label this “white space” yet)
	•	A simple coverage meter (e.g., 2 of 5 covered)

Use compact, non-tabular visuals like:
	•	Radial charts, horizontal bars, or visual meters
	•	Color-coded indicators (e.g., green = covered, gray = uncovered)

Add a fit indicator if possible: “Customer profile suggests 4 of 5 products are relevant.”

⸻

2. 🟥 Policies Requiring Attention

A clearly scoped section showing contracts that are expiring soon or flagged for action.
	•	Visual timeline bar or stacked cards
	•	Each card should show:
	•	Product name
	•	End date (e.g., “Expires in 19 days”)
	•	Suggested action (e.g., “Renew,” “Upgrade,” “Bundle”)
	•	Expected value impact (e.g., “€1,800 uplift if upgraded to Premium”)

Use urgency indicators like 🔴 ⚠️ and urgency labels like “Act within 30 days.”

⸻

3. 🟨 Coverage Expansion Paths (Strategic Cross-Sell)

Present a prioritized view of additional products to propose — not in a list by category, but grouped by:
	•	💰 High Value
	•	⏰ Time-Sensitive
	•	✅ Profile Fit / Easy Win

Each card should include:
	•	Product name
	•	Why it’s relevant (e.g., “Customer has Auto + Home, 78% of similar profiles add Legal”)
	•	Estimated premium value
	•	CTA: Prepare Quote, Schedule Call, or Send Offer

Use insurance lingo like:
	•	“Add coverage” instead of “Cross-sell”
	•	“Exposure” instead of “Gap”
	•	“Premium uplift” instead of “Potential revenue”

⸻

4. 🟩 Total Premium Impact Panel

At the bottom or pinned to the side:
	•	“Estimated premium uplift from recommended actions: €9,300 / year”
	•	Optionally: % growth vs current portfolio

Helps the manager quantify impact.

⸻

🎨 Design Language & Components
	•	Use shadcn/ui components
	•	Layout should feel like a compliance-grade, Apple-style interface:
	•	Clean
	•	Hierarchical
	•	Padded and precise
	•	Avoid overwhelming color: focus on signal, not noise
	•	Prioritize scan-ability and decision-making — no digging or mental comparison


⸻

✅ Success =

A strategic insurance account manager opens this interface and immediately understands:
	•	Where the customer is well covered
	•	Where there’s risk or renewal urgency
	•	What coverage to add next
	•	What it’s worth — without having to analyze anything
